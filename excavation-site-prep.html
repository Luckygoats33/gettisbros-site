Land Clearing

Land Clearing & Grading

Custom grading tailored to your property's unique conditions.

Our grading and land-clearing services are customized to each property's layout, soil type, and drainage needs. We remove vegetation and debris, level uneven surfaces, and establish proper slope to promote stability and prevent erosion — an essential step for building along the Oregon Coast.

Proper grading is the foundation of every successful construction project. Our team ensures your site is prepared to handle the unique challenges of coastal terrain, from steep grades to sandy soil conditions.

Foundation Prep

Driveway & Foundation Preparation

A strong subgrade for lasting structural reliability.

Before any concrete or asphalt is poured, a well-compacted and properly graded base is critical. Gettis Bros creates a strong subgrade for driveways, foundations, patios, and roads, ensuring structural reliability and resilience against coastal weather, heavy rain, and shifting soil.

Our foundation prep work sets the stage for everything that follows. We take the time to get it right, because a solid foundation means fewer problems down the road — saving you time, money, and headaches for years to come.
