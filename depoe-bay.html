Construction Services in Depoe Bay, Oregon

Quality construction for Depoe Bay's stunning cliffside properties and coastal community

Your Trusted Depoe Bay Construction Contractor

Depoe Bay is one of the most visually dramatic communities on the entire Oregon Coast. Home to the world's smallest navigable harbor, towering basalt cliffs, and the famous spouting horns that launch ocean spray high into the air, this town demands construction work that matches its extraordinary landscape. Gettis Bros has been building and improving properties in Depoe Bay for decades, and we understand that every project here comes with unique challenges that only experienced local contractors can navigate.

Building on Depoe Bay's cliffside lots requires specialized knowledge of foundation engineering, proper drainage systems, and erosion control. Many of the town's upscale vacation homes and year-round residences sit perched above the Pacific Ocean, exposed to relentless salt spray, high winds, and driving rain. From driveways along Highway 101 to patios overlooking the whale watching grounds, we select materials and construction methods specifically engineered to withstand Depoe Bay's extreme coastal conditions while delivering the premium finish these properties deserve.

Whether you own a cliffside home with panoramic ocean views, a commercial property near the Whale Watching Center, or a charter fishing business down by the harbor, Gettis Bros brings the same fourth-generation craftsmanship to every Depoe Bay project. We handle everything from asphalt paving and sealcoating to concrete hardscaping, excavation, fencing, sheds, and ground-up new construction. As a CCB licensed and insured contractor with deep roots in Lincoln County, we are committed to building structures that endure the coastal elements for years to come.
