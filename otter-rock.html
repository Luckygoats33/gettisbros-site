Otter Rock Construction Services

Expert construction services for Otter Rock's scenic headland properties

Your Trusted Contractor in Otter Rock, Oregon

Perched on a dramatic headland between Depoe Bay and Newport, Otter Rock is one of the Oregon Coast's most striking communities. Home to Devil's Punchbowl State Natural Area -- where a collapsed sea cave churns with every incoming wave -- this small unincorporated neighborhood offers ocean views that few places on the coast can match. From the vacation condominiums at the Inn at Otter Crest to the permanent residences lining the bluffs above Beverly Beach, properties here sit in one of the most visually spectacular and environmentally demanding locations in Lincoln County. Gettis Bros has the expertise to build and maintain structures in this uniquely exposed coastal setting.

Construction in Otter Rock is not the same as building a few miles inland. Properties on the headland face the full force of Pacific storms -- driving rain, sustained winds, and salt spray that can corrode unprotected materials within a single season. Every driveway, retaining wall, fence, and foundation we build here is engineered with that exposure in mind. We select corrosion-resistant materials, design proper drainage to handle heavy runoff on sloped terrain, and anchor structures to withstand the wind loads that come with oceanfront and ocean-view building sites along the Otter Crest Loop.

Whether you own a vacation rental that needs upgraded outdoor living spaces, a permanent residence requiring a new concrete patio with an ocean view, or an undeveloped lot along the scenic loop road that needs excavation and site preparation, Gettis Bros delivers fourth-generation craftsmanship tailored to Otter Rock's conditions. We understand the balance between building for durability and preserving the dramatic natural beauty that makes this community special. Every project we take on is designed to complement the landscape, not compete with it.
