Our Services

At Gettis Bros., we deliver dependable, high-quality work across the Oregon Coast. From asphalt and concrete to fencing and new construction, our team combines craftsmanship, reliability, and local expertise.

Asphalt & Sealcoating

Durable, clean, and coast-tough surfaces.

We specialize in asphalt paving, patching, sealcoating, and parking lot striping — built to handle Oregon’s coastal conditions and deliver long-lasting results for driveways, roads, and commercial lots.

Our premium sealcoating process forms a durable barrier against UV rays, moisture, and salt corrosion. Regular sealcoating keeps pavement flexible, prevents cracking, and maintains a dark, clean appearance.
