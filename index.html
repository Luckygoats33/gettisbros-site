Our Heritage

Four Generations. One Family. Built for the Coast.

A Family Tradition of Building Right

The Gettis family has been shaping Lincoln County since the early 1960s. What started as a small family operation has grown into a trusted name in coastal construction — passed down through four generations of builders who know this land inside and out.

We didn't learn our trade from a textbook. We learned it from our fathers and grandfathers, working side by side on the Oregon Coast. That hands-on legacy means every project we take on carries the weight of 60+ years of family pride and coastal expertise.

When you hire Gettis Bros, you're not hiring a faceless company. You're working with the family that built its reputation one project at a time, right here in your community.
