ABOUT US

Gettis Bros Asphalt & Construction was founded in 2012, but our roots go back much further. Our story began in the early 1960s when my great-grandfather moved to the Oregon Coast from Salem and began working in construction. Since then, four generations of our family have built, repaired, and maintained homes and infrastructure up and down Lincoln County.

We know this land, this weather, and this community -- because it's home.

OUR MISSION

To deliver quality construction and hardscaping services built on trust, craftsmanship, and lasting results for every client.

OUR VISION

To be the Oregon Coast's most trusted name in construction -- known for reliability, precision, and community pride.

OUR VALUE

We stand by integrity, hard work, and respect -- ensuring every project reflects our commitment to excellence.

ASPHALT & SEALCOATING
DURABLE, CLEAN, AND COAST-TOUGH SURFACES.

COAST-TOUGH ASPHALT & SEALCOATING BUILT TO ENDURE

At Gettis Bros, we deliver professional asphalt paving, patching, sealcoating, and parking-lot striping built specifically for the Oregon Coast's demanding conditions. Serving Lincoln City, Newport, and surrounding coastal communities, our team creates smooth, long-lasting surfaces that withstand wind, rain, and salt air--perfect for driveways, roads, and commercial lots.

EXPERT ASPHALT PAVING & MAINTENANCE

From new installations to resurfacing and repairs, Gettis Bros manages every phase of your asphalt project with precision. We use high-grade materials and modern equipment to ensure strength, stability, and a flawless finish. Each job is engineered to handle Oregon's variable weather and heavy coastal moisture while maintaining curb appeal year-round.

SEALCOATING & SURFACE PROTECTION

Protecting your asphalt is key to extending its life. Our premium sealcoating process forms a durable barrier against UV rays, moisture, and salt corrosion--a must for coastal climates. Regular sealcoating keeps pavement flexible, prevents cracking, and maintains a dark, clean appearance that enhances your property's value.

ASPHALT REPAIR & PATCHING SOLUTIONS

Cracks and potholes don't stand a chance. Our repair specialists quickly restore smoothness and structural integrity to damaged surfaces, preventing water intrusion and costly replacements. Whether it's a small patch or a full-section restoration, we get your pavement back to peak performance.

PARKING LOT STRIPING & FINISHING TOUCH

Fresh, accurate striping brings both function and professionalism to your property. Gettis Bros provides high-visibility line marking for parking lots, roads, and driveways that meets safety standards and complements your pavement's clean finish.
