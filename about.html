About Gettis Bros

Our Story

Built for the Coast. Backed by Generations.

Gettis Bros Asphalt & Construction was founded in 2012, but our roots go back much further. Our story began in the early 1960s when my great-grandfather moved to the Oregon Coast from Salem and began working in construction. Since then, four generations of our family have built, repaired, and maintained homes and infrastructure up and down Lincoln County.

We know this land, this weather, and this community — because it’s home.

Gettis Bros is more than a business — it’s a family legacy. Since the beginning, our focus has been on delivering quality work with reliable timelines, honest communication, and a strong sense of accountability. From sealcoating a residential driveway to excavating land for a new home, we treat every project like it’s on our own property.

Coastal Expertise

Why Coastal Construction Requires Experience

Oregon’s coastal climate creates unique challenges — moisture, salt air, and shifting ground conditions all demand a smarter, more resilient approach. That’s why we use materials and methods specifically designed to stand up to the coast’s wear and tear.

  • Moisture-resistant materials and drainage solutions
  • Salt-air corrosion protection on all metal components
  • Foundation techniques for shifting coastal soil
  • Wind-rated structures built to withstand coastal storms
  • 60+ years of local climate knowledge
